Global Chinatowns

Over centuries of migration, Chinese communities have enclaves known as Chinatowns in cities around the world, on nearly every continent. The history of Chinatowns began in the Tang dynasty in the 10th century, which is reflected in the Chinese names for Chinatowns 唐人街 - the streets of the Tang people.

This map traces all the Chinatowns around the world listed on Wikipedia, exploring patterns of migrations and the spatial and physical presence that Chinese migrants have left in the cities that they've settled in.
